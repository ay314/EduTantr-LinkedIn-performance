# EduTantr LinkedIn Performance Dashboard

An interactive dashboard that turns LinkedIn Analytics exports into a visual view of how an EdTech company page performed. It's part of my LinkedIn brand management and content marketing project for EduTantr (Next Gen EduTantr IT Solutions).

**Live demo:** [add your hosted link here]

## About the project

I managed EduTantr's LinkedIn company page, planning and writing content for students and graduates and reviewing post analytics. This dashboard presents that analysis in one interactive view.

- **Period:** 1 Sep – 15 Oct 2026 (UTC)
- **Scope:** 22 organic posts, 45 days of account-level data
- **Source:** LinkedIn Analytics export (organic only, no paid promotion)

## Features

- Filter the whole dashboard by content category: placement stories, calendar and awareness, career guidance, skill development, company recognition
- Daily impressions chart with every post marked as a dot
- Donut chart showing the share of impressions by category
- Bubble chart comparing reach, click rate and reactions for each post
- Post leaderboard switchable between impressions, clicks and reactions
- Hover and tap tooltips, animated counters, and light and dark themes

## Key findings

- Two placement-story posts were 9% of all posts but earned 39% of post impressions.
- Calendar posts earned more reactions on average (15.5) but very few clicks, while career-guidance posts earned a 40% click rate with fewer reactions (7.7).
- Daily impressions averaged 947 in September and 409 in October. October had no placement stories, and the newest posts were still collecting views.
- The 22 posts drew about 8 comments in total.

## Notes on the data

- Click rate is clicks divided by impressions. LinkedIn counts "see more" expansions as clicks.
- The daily chart shows account-wide impressions (34,530), which include views of older posts. The headline cards use post-level totals (19,694).
- Post categories are my own grouping based on each post's topic.

## Tech

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with inline SVG charts. There are no frameworks or build steps, and it's a single file.

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in any browser.
